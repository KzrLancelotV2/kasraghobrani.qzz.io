About Me

I am a Computer Engineering undergraduate at the University of Tehran. Born in December 2003, I graduated from Mofid High School before beginning my studies in computer engineering.

With the current AI boom and the prospect of artificial general intelligence (AGI), I think AI for biology could be the next big wave. What matters to me is where that takes us: helping people live healthier, better, and longer lives. I want to be part of making that happen.

Outside of academics, I enjoy traveling, learning languages, reading history, and going to the gym. I try to keep up with politics and understand what's happening around the world. I believe in equal opportunity for everyone and solidarity with people facing oppression, regardless of their beliefs, skin color, or race.
